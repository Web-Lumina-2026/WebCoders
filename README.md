# CULTURE.EXE — Remix the Scene

**A digital culture experience exploring how internet aesthetics, fashion, identity, and emerging trends shape the world around us.**

## ✦ About the Project

**CULTURE.EXE** is an experimental digital culture website built around the idea that culture is constantly evolving, remixing, and reinventing itself.

From internet-born aesthetics to real-world fashion movements, the platform explores how digital trends influence personal identity, creative expression, and modern lifestyles.

Designed under the concept **“Remix the Scene,”** CULTURE.EXE combines bold editorial layouts, immersive video visuals, curated imagery, and interactive elements to create a distinctive digital experience.

Rather than presenting culture as something static, the website treats it as an evolving system of ideas, aesthetics, and influences.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Explore the relationship between digital culture and real-world expression.
- Present emerging aesthetics and cultural movements through an engaging visual experience.
- Combine editorial storytelling with interactive web design.
- Create a distinctive, immersive, and visually cohesive website.
- Encourage users to discover, interpret, and experiment with cultural identities.

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

CULTURE.EXE follows a digital-editorial design approach inspired by contemporary fashion, internet aesthetics, and experimental visual culture.

**Core design principles:**

- **Bold visual identity:** Strong typography and expressive layouts.
- **Immersive media:** Video-led sections and curated imagery.
- **Editorial storytelling:** Content presented as a visual narrative rather than a conventional information page.
- **Interactive discovery:** Browsing, filtering, and persona-based exploration.
- **Visual consistency:** A unified design language across the website.
- **Digital experimentation:** A blend of online aesthetics and offline cultural influences.
